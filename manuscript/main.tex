\begin{abstract}
Large Language Models (LLMs) show promise for clinical decision support but pose risks through factual errors or "hallucinations." This study evaluates hallucination rates when using GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini to interpret proteomics data. We find significant variation across models and identify key risk factors for clinical deployment.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} Large Language Models, Hallucination, Proteomics, Clinical AI, Healthcare Safety
