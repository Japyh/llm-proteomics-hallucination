# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of this academic research project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, professional, and ethical experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy research community committed to scientific integrity and ethical conduct.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the overall research community and project
- Maintaining academic integrity and honesty
- Respecting confidentiality and data privacy
- Properly citing sources and giving credit where due

### Academic and Research Ethics

Participants in this project are expected to:

- **Maintain Research Integrity**: Never fabricate or falsify data
- **Respect Privacy**: Strictly adhere to GDPR and patient privacy regulations
- **Cite Properly**: Give appropriate credit to others' work
- **Avoid Plagiarism**: Always attribute ideas and content to original authors
- **Disclose Conflicts**: Be transparent about potential conflicts of interest
- **Share Responsibly**: Share data and findings according to ethical guidelines
- **Protect Participants**: Never use real patient data without proper consent
- **Follow Protocols**: Adhere to IRB guidelines and ethical review processes

### Data Privacy and Security

- NEVER commit real patient data to version control
- NEVER share protected health information (PHI) or personally identifiable information (PII)
- Always anonymize data before processing
- Secure API keys and credentials
- Report data breaches immediately
- Follow GDPR and local data protection regulations

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Academic dishonesty including plagiarism, data fabrication, or misrepresentation
- Unauthorized use of patient data or breach of confidentiality
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repository
- Issue tracker
- Pull requests
- Code reviews
- Email communications
- Project meetings (online or in-person)
- Conference presentations
- Academic publications

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at:

- **Project Lead**: olaflaitinen (contact via GitHub)
- **Collaborator**: Japyh (contact via GitHub)

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

### Data Privacy Violations

Data privacy violations are particularly serious and should be reported immediately:

1. Contact project maintainers immediately
2. Do NOT attempt to access, copy, or redistribute the data
3. Document the incident (what, when, where)
4. Follow institutional data breach protocols

### Academic Integrity Violations

Cases of suspected academic misconduct (plagiarism, data fabrication, etc.) should be reported to project leads and may be escalated to institutional review boards or ethics committees as appropriate.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from project maintainers, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in project spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior or academic misconduct.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals. This also includes serious violations of research ethics or data privacy.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the project community.

## Research-Specific Guidelines

### Clinical Data and Patient Privacy

Working with clinical data, even synthetic or anonymized data, requires special care:

- Always assume data might be identifiable
- Never attempt to re-identify anonymized data
- Follow all applicable regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, local laws)
- Obtain proper approvals before data collection
- Maintain secure storage and transmission practices
- Report any suspected privacy breaches immediately

### Publication and Attribution

- Discuss authorship openly and early in the project
- Follow established authorship guidelines (ICMJE criteria)
- Acknowledge all significant contributions
- Share credit appropriately
- Respect intellectual property rights

### Reproducibility and Transparency

- Document methods clearly and completely
- Share code and analysis scripts when possible
- Maintain version control and audit trails
- Be transparent about limitations and assumptions
- Make data available when ethically and legally permissible
