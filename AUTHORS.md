## How to Get Listed

### For Code Contributors

If you contribute code:
1. Submit a pull request
2. Your contribution will be reviewed
3. Upon merge, you will be added to this file
4. Significant contributions may warrant co-authorship (see CONTRIBUTING.md)

### For Research Collaborators

If you contribute to research:
1. Contact the Principal Investigator (olyulaim@dtu.dk)
2. Discuss your contribution
3. Formal collaboration agreement if appropriate
4. Attribution according to ICMJE guidelines

## Authorship Criteria

We follow the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) criteria for authorship:

**Authorship requires**:
1. Substantial contributions to conception/design or data acquisition/analysis
2. Drafting the work or revising it critically
3. Final approval of the version to be published
4. Agreement to be accountable for all aspects of the work

**All criteria must be met** for authorship.
