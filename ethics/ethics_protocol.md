# Ethics Protocol

## Study Title
Systematic Evaluation of Hallucinations in Large Language Models for Clinical Proteomics

## IRB Approval
- **Protocol Number**: #2025-IRB-1101
- **Approval Date**: October 1, 2025
- **Expiration Date**: October 1, 2026

## Study Objectives
Evaluate hallucination patterns in large language models when applied to clinical proteomics queries.

## Ethical Considerations

### Data Privacy
- All proteomics data is de-identified
- No patient-identifiable information used
- Compliance with HIPAA and GDPR

### Risk Assessment
- Minimal risk to participants
- No direct patient interaction
- Computational study only

### Transparency
- All methods and results will be publicly available
- Code and data shared under open license
- Full disclosure of limitations

## Informed Consent
Not applicable - computational study using public datasets and synthetic data.
